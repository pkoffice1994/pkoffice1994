## 🛡️ AI Governance & Responsible AI

I believe enterprise AI should be **secure, accountable, explainable and reliable** — not just powerful. I focus on connecting governance principles with practical controls across the AI lifecycle, especially for **agentic AI systems**.

| Governance priority | Practical focus |
|:---|:---|
| **AI Inventory & Accountability** | Identify AI systems, owners, intended use and decision rights |
| **Risk Assessment** | Classify use cases by impact, sensitivity and required safeguards |
| **Data Privacy & Security** | Protect sensitive information, manage permissions and limit data exposure |
| **Agent Runtime Controls** | Check authority before actions, enforce approvals and support emergency stops |
| **Human Oversight** | Define review points, escalation paths and human intervention |
| **Verification & Evaluations** | Test accuracy, prohibited actions, edge cases and failure scenarios |
| **Monitoring & Auditability** | Capture evidence, investigate incidents and continuously improve controls |

**Frameworks I follow and explore:** NIST AI RMF · ISO/IEC 42001 · EU AI Act — applied as relevant to organizational needs and jurisdiction.

> **My perspective:** AI governance should not end with a policy document. Controls need to work when AI agents make decisions and take actions.
